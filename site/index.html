Compatibility without pretending

A clear signal,
not a magic wand.

Open Nevada tracks the contracts a mod needs, validates them against real launches, and publishes the result. If something is missing, we show the gap — we do not hide it behind a green button.

  1. 01
    Isolate

    Profiles never overwrite a game installation, save folder, or official conversion output.

  2. 02
    Measure

    Telemetry records what a runtime or mod actually asks for during a launch.

  3. 03
    Promote

    Support is earned by reproducible validation, on each platform that claims it.
