# XR weapon actions

OpenXR fire and reload enter the ordinary native weapon owner as named semantic
actions. They do not synthesize a fixed desktop mouse button or R key. The
player's source control map can replace or omit those events without changing
the meaning of a tracked controller action.

The existing weapon owner still enforces source fighting enablement, modal and
furniture input, trigger edges, reload timing, ammunition and authored animation
events. Flat input continues through its configured source bindings. No separate
VR damage, ammunition or animation state is introduced.

The native owned-data fixture removes both desktop action event lists, reloads
the selected source laser pistol, verifies the source fighting refusal and
fires exactly one round despite a repeated pressed value. It checks release,
ammunition conservation and firing/effect errors. This isolated fixture does not
establish ordinary campaign combat, retail parity or physical-headset acceptance;
those require their independent gameplay and final-eye evidence.

An ordinary FNV simulator run cold-loads its genuine exterior checkpoint,
equips the owned laser pistol through wrist inventory, reloads through the right
controller, walks around a blocking porch fence and kills a resident source crow
through tracked trigger input. The retained left-eye video shows both hands,
weapon and fallen body. The original checkpoint remains unchanged. Source
material/LOD failures, unsupported muzzle-light semantics and broader combat
behavior remain separate visible divergences.
