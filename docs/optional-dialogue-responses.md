even when their response collection is empty. The reader no longer requires a
TRDT/NAM1 pair in every INFO. It preserves zero responses rather than inventing
a silent voice, response number, duration or text. Source selection evaluates
the authored conditions and order normally.

separate runtime owner. That boundary is explicit in selection diagnostics.
